# Target Markets & Use Cases

## Overview

letstalkAI transforms any PDF document into a conversational knowledge base with intelligent image understanding. The app leverages Apple Intelligence for on-device AI processing, making it ideal for privacy-sensitive industries and personal use.

---

## Enterprise Markets (B2B)

### 1. Automotive Industry
**Use Case:** Sales teams and dealerships

| Scenario | Query Example |
|----------|---------------|
| Product demonstrations | "Show me the interior features" |
| Specification lookup | "What's the engine horsepower?" |
| Comparison shopping | "What safety features are included?" |
| Visual presentations | "Display all exterior color options" |

**Value Proposition:**
- Sales reps can instantly answer customer questions
- No need to memorize every specification
- Visual responses enhance customer engagement
